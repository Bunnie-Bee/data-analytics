## Introduction

## What I’ll Explore

- **Data Import & Transformation** – Using Power Query to connect to various data sources, clean, shape, and combine data.
- **Data Modeling** – Creating relationships, calculated columns, and measures with DAX (Data Analysis Expressions).
- **Interactive Visualizations** – Building dashboards with rich visuals, slicers, drill‑throughs, and custom visuals.
- **Publishing & Collaboration** – Sharing reports on the Power BI Service, setting up workspaces, and collaborating with stakeholders.
- **Advanced Features** – Exploring Row‑Level Security, paginated reports, and AI insights.

I will document each milestone, share screenshots, and reflect on challenges along the way.
